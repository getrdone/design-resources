# Color library conventions

- Top-level materials gallery folder is named `# materials` (sorts first).
- When adding any new **idea library** under `themes/` (download import or generated), always place `{theme}--materials-mood--01.png` in `# materials/` and keep `# materials/{theme}/MATERIALS.txt`.
- When adding a **named theme** (locked brief / expanding catalog), put it under `../color-themes/<theme-kebab>/` and still mirror its materials mood into `# materials/`.
- Do not mix the two: `themes/` = idea libraries; `color-themes/` = named systems.
